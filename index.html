The Farm

A dairy farm that
refused to disappear

For its first century this was a working dairy. Fifty years ago the owners before us let the pasture go to pine and walked away from the barns. In November 2020 we bought the 63-acre tract, sat down with Mississippi State's Agricultural Extension, and started putting it back — carefully, and on the land's terms.

The wood barn has a loft in it now. The cinder-block dairy shed is a guest cottage. Twelve acres of wild growth came out and Bermuda grass went in, fenced in steel and finished in 2021. There is a raised herb and vegetable garden, an orchard, and a chicken coop.

A Day Here

There is nothing to do.
That took a lot of work.

6:40

Coffee on the porch, and the birds start it

The birdhouses are up — three for wrens or bluebirds, one for a screech owl, another for chickadees. Bring binoculars. If the season is right you will catch the mating dance, which is exactly as ridiculous as it sounds.

2:15

Under the oak, doing absolutely nothing

The live oak between the three buildings is certified by an arborist at over a hundred years old. It is the centre of the compound and the best shade in Walthall County. There are chairs. Use them.

7:05

Grill, baby. Grill.

South Mississippi does a particular kind of sunset — the sort that makes people stop mid-sentence. Light the pit, put something on it, and watch the colour go out of the sky over twelve acres of Bermuda grass.

9:30

The barn lights come on and that is the whole show

No traffic. No streetlights. Two miles north of the Louisiana line there is very little between you and the stars, and the horses have already settled.

The Dance

This is what
we mean by slow

Twenty-two seconds of two birds working it out on the fence line. The big live oak is a nesting haven and more birdhouses are going in to widen the habitat. Turkeys and deer turn up seasonally. Bring binoculars — it is all seasonally sensitive, and early is better.

The Trails

Trails meander through the woods and were recently re-mulched. Two park benches sit in the western corners of the pasture for when you have gone far enough.

Cloud Dreaming

Pareidolia: seeing meaning in a shapeless thing. Here we just call it cloud dreaming, and most of them come out as dogs. Bring polarising sunglasses.

Doing Nothing, Properly

There are recliners. There is a porch. There is a cat who has this figured out better than any of us and is happy to demonstrate.

Grillin' On The Pit

Eat, Drink & Enjoy

Around the block,
Mississippi-style

South of Tylertown, north of Franklinton, two miles up from the Louisiana line — which puts a surprising amount within an easy drive.

Tylertown, MS

Sweetwater Canoe & Tubing

Canoe, kayak and tube rentals for the Bogue Chitto and Magee's Creek, seven days a week March through December. In winter there is a fire, and a decent chance of deer or wild turkey.

Folsom, LA

Global Wildlife Center

A 900-acre private preserve you tour by wagon. Free-roaming bison, giraffes, eland, zebra and camels — an hour-plus that children and adults both fail to get bored of.

October · Franklinton, LA

Washington Parish Free Fair

Running since 1911 and believed on attendance to be the largest free county fair in the USA. Livestock, cut flowers, Old McDonald's Farm, the Midway and a PRO Rodeo.

Bogue Chitto, LA

Bogue Chitto State Park

1,786 acres of cypress-tupelo swamp, hardwood and upland forest. Water park, hiking and horse trails, eleven fishing lakes, camping, cabins and tubing.

Tylertown & Franklinton

Where To Eat

Lagniappe Coffee House, Stogner & Son Fish Camp and Los Parrilleros in Tylertown. Red Brick Cafe, Bo's Steakhouse, Angkor Village and the Pine Malt Stand in Franklinton.

Sale day · Tuesday

The Stockyard

The Livestock Producers Association yard runs its sale every Tuesday. If you have never sat through a livestock auction, it is free entertainment and a genuine slice of the place.
